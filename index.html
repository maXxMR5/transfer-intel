Methodology and safeguards

Canonical movements

One row represents one player movement. Duplicate club-perspective records are removed during database construction.

Bidirectional definition

A route qualifies only where at least one eligible movement exists in each direction between two real clubs.

Entity exclusions

Administrative states such as “Without Club”, “Retired”, “Career break”, “Free agent” and unknown placeholders are excluded from route rankings.

Fee caution

Reported fees are estimates, not verified contractual amounts. Zero and missing values retain separate confidence labels.
