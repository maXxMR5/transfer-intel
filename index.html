Trust and transparency

About the data

How the rankings are built, what is excluded, and where caution is needed.

What counts as a route?

One canonical row represents one player movement. A route qualifies only when at least one movement exists in each direction between two genuine clubs.

What is excluded?

Administrative states such as Without Club, Retired, Career break, Unknown and similar placeholders are retained in the raw transfer history but excluded from every club relationship ranking.

Fees and loans

Reported fees are estimates, not verified contractual amounts. Zero and missing values retain separate confidence labels. Loans are identified independently and shown explicitly.

Quality checks

Routes are checked for duplicate pairs, self-transfers, both-direction activity, reconciled totals and invalid non-club endpoints before publication.
