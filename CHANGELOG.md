# Changelog

## Transfer Mates v0.1.0 Beta — RC1

### Added
- Transfer Mates branding and public-beta presentation.
- Country-first club selectors, grouped into England, Spain, Germany, Italy, France and Other clubs.
- Rich route summary cards with direction split, unique players, loans, non-loans, balance and activity span.
- Improved loading, empty and error states.
- Mobile-first layout and horizontally scrollable analytical tables.
- Expanded About the data section and visible quality methodology.
- Browser-side validation that stops the app if non-club entities enter rankings or route totals do not reconcile.

### Changed
- Re-ranked all eligible bidirectional routes after exclusions.
- Improved route and league tables, transfer type badges and fee confidence labels.
- Club selectors now contain only clubs participating in an eligible bidirectional route.

### Fixed
- Excluded administrative states such as Without Club, Retired, Career break, Unknown and placeholders from club-to-club relationship rankings.
- Removed 100 ineligible routes from the public analysis.
