Methodology and safeguards

One canonical row represents one player movement. Duplicate club-perspective records are removed during database construction. Routes qualify as bidirectional only when at least one movement exists in each direction.

Reported fees are not treated as verified contractual amounts. Zero and missing values retain separate confidence labels. Every published row has a stable transfer UID and derives from the quality-checked SQLite warehouse.
